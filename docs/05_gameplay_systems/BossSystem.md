**Document Version:** 0.1.0

# Purpose

The Boss System defines powerful enemies that provide memorable combat experiences and high-value rewards.

The objective is to create encounters that challenge players through:

- Strategy.
- Team coordination.
- Preparation.
- Adaptation.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Bosses Are Experiences

A boss should not only be a stronger enemy.

A great boss provides:

```
Identity

+

Challenge

+

Story

+

Mechanics

+

Rewards
```

# Dungeon Bosses

Bosses located inside structured content.

Purpose:

- Complete dungeon experiences.
- Provide progression rewards.

---

## Characteristics

Dungeon bosses include:

- Unique mechanics.
- Multiple attacks.
- Special loot tables.

---

# Elite Bosses

Rare enemies found in the world.

Characteristics:

- Stronger than normal creatures.
- Better rewards.
- Exploration incentives.

---

# World Bosses

Large-scale enemies available to many players.

Purpose:

Create community events.

Example:

```
Ancient Dragon Appears

↓

Players From Multiple Regions Join

↓

Global Battle
```

---

# Raid Bosses

Designed for large groups.

Requirements:

- Team coordination.
- Specialized strategies.
- High preparation.

---

# Legendary Bosses

Endgame encounters.

Characteristics:

- Extremely difficult.
- Unique rewards.
- World-changing impact.

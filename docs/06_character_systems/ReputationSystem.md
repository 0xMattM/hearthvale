# Reputation System

**Document Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Work in Progress  
**Owner:** Game Design Team  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

---

# Purpose

The Reputation System defines how player actions create a long-term social identity inside the world.

The system allows players to build recognition through:

- Economic activity.
- Social behavior.
- Professional achievements.
- Community participation.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Reputation Is Social Value

Reputation is not a power stat.

It represents:

- Trust.
- Experience.
- Recognition.
- History.

---

# Reputation Goals

The system should help players identify:

- Reliable traders.
- Skilled producers.
- Experienced fighters.
- Valuable community members.

---

# Reputation Categories

Player reputation is divided into different areas.

```

+
```

---

# Economic Reputation

Represents player activity in the economy.

Based on:

- Successful trades.
- Marketplace activity.
- Production history.
- Commercial reliability.

---

# Economic Reputation Benefits

Possible benefits:

- Recognition as a merchant.
- Better visibility in marketplace.
- Public rankings.
- Titles.

---

# Profession Reputation

Each profession has independent reputation.

Examples:

```
Master Farmer

Expert Blacksmith

Elite Hunter

Skilled Carpenter
```

---

# Profession Reputation Sources

Earned through:

- Producing items.
- Completing activities.
- Crafting quality products.
- Helping other players.

---

# Quality Reputation

Example:

Experienced blacksmith:

↓

---

# Avoiding Reputation Abuse

The system must prevent:

- Fake ratings.
- Player manipulation.
- Artificial reputation farming.

Possible solutions:

- Limit repeated interactions.
- Require real activity.
- Track behavior patterns.

---

# Reputation And Economy

Reputation creates economic advantages.

Example:

Two players sell swords.

Player A:

- Unknown.
- No history.

Player B:

- Famous blacksmith.
- High reputation.

Players may choose Player B because of trust.

# Reputation Decay

Possible future mechanic.

Inactive reputation may slowly lose visibility.

Purpose:

Keep the world dynamic.

---

# Blockchain Relationship

Reputation should mostly remain off-chain.

Reason:

- Frequent updates.
- Lower cost.
- Better flexibility.

Possible blockchain usage:

- Achievement proofs.
- Rare titles.
- Special badges.

---

# Three.js Considerations

Visual representation:

- Titles above players.
- Guild symbols.
- Achievement effects.

---

# Balance Rules

Reputation should:

- Reward long-term behavior.
- Encourage quality.
- Create identity.
- Not block new players.

---

# Final Statement

The Reputation System gives economic and social value to player history.

In a world controlled by players, trust becomes an important resource that influences trade, cooperation, and community growth.

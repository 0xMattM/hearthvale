# Level System

**Document Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Work in Progress  
**Owner:** Game Design Team  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

---

# Purpose

The Level System defines how players measure their overall progression in the world.

Player Level represents general experience gained from participating in the game's ecosystem.

It is separate from Profession Levels, which represent specialization in specific activities.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Level Represents Experience, Not Power

A higher player level means:

- More experienced.
- More active.
- More familiar with the world.

It does not automatically mean:

- Stronger combat ability.
- Higher economic success.
- Better production in every profession.

---

## Multiple Paths To Progression

Players gain levels through many activities.

Examples:

- Farming.
- Mining.
- Crafting.
- Trading.
- Hunting.
- Construction.
- Exploration.

There is no single optimal way to level.

---

# Level Structure

The player has:

```
Player Level

+

Profession Levels
```

Both systems progress independently.

---

# Player Level

Represents overall character experience.

---

# Experience Sources

Player XP is gained from:

## Gathering

Examples:

- Mining resources.
- Cutting trees.
- Harvesting crops.

---

## Crafting

Examples:

- Creating tools.
- Creating equipment.
- Creating furniture.

---

## Construction

Examples:

- Building structures.
- Improving land.

---

## Combat

Examples:

- Defeating monsters.
- Completing dangerous activities.

---

## Exploration

Examples:

- Discovering regions.
- Finding resources.

---

# Level Requirements

The XP required increases gradually.

Example:

```
Level 1 → 2

Low requirement


Level 20 → 21

Higher requirement


Level 80 → 81

Very high requirement
```

The curve should reward new players while providing long-term goals.

---

# Level Rewards

Player levels unlock opportunities.

Possible rewards:

- New features.
- New regions.
- Cosmetic rewards.
- Additional customization.
- Access to advanced systems.

---

# Avoiding Power Gaps

Player level should not create extreme advantages.

A level 100 player should not automatically defeat a level 20 player.

Experience creates opportunities, not dominance.

---

# Profession Relationship

Player Level is calculated from all activities.

Example:

Player:

```
Farming Level 30

Mining Level 10

Blacksmith Level 20
```

May have:

```
Player Level 35
```

---

# Profession Levels

Profession levels are separate.

Examples:

- Farming Level.
- Mining Level.
- Blacksmith Level.
- Cooking Level.
- Hunting Level.

---

# Why Separate Systems Exist

Without separation:

A player could:

Farm thousands of crops.

↓

Become maximum level.

↓

Automatically become powerful in combat.

This would damage specialization.

---

# Level Display

Players should see:

- Current level.
- Current XP.
- XP required.
- Recent XP gains.

---

# Level Milestones

Important milestones create motivation.

Examples:

Level 5:

- Access new recipes.

Level 10:

- Unlock advanced systems.

Level 25:

- Access specialized content.

Level 50:

- Prestige opportunities.

---

# Level Progression Example

A new player:

```
Player Level 1

Farming Level 1

Mining Level 1
```

After several weeks:

```
Player Level 25

Farming Level 40

Mining Level 12

Cooking Level 15
```

The player's identity emerges naturally.

---

# Experience Distribution

Different activities provide different amounts of XP.

The reward should consider:

- Time required.
- Energy consumed.
- Economic value created.
- Difficulty.

---

# Level Exploitation Prevention

The system should prevent:

- Repeating meaningless actions.
- Creating infinite XP loops.
- Abusing low-cost activities.

---

# Relationship With Energy

Energy limits how much progression a player can generate.

This prevents unlimited production and XP farming.

---

# Relationship With Economy

Higher-level players should contribute more value.

Examples:

- Creating advanced items.
- Unlocking new production chains.
- Helping economic growth.

---

# Future Expansion

Possible additions:

- Titles.
- Achievements.
- Reputation.
- Mastery ranks.
- Seasonal progression.

---

# Final Statement

The Level System represents the player's journey through the world.

It rewards participation, exploration, and contribution while allowing every player to develop a unique identity through their chosen activities.

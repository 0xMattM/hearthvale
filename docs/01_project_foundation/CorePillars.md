# Purpose

This document defines the immutable design principles of the game.

Core Pillars are not gameplay mechanics.

They are the fundamental beliefs that define the identity of the project.

Every feature, profession, building, item, blockchain mechanic, combat system, or expansion must reinforce these pillars.

If a future feature contradicts one or more pillars, the feature must be redesigned or rejected.

---

# Pillar 01 â€” Player Driven Economy

## Statement

The economy belongs to the players.

Players create value.

Players transform value.

Players exchange value.

Players determine value.

The game provides the systems.

The community creates the economy.

---

## Design Goals

- Encourage player interaction.
- Encourage specialization.
- Encourage trade.
- Create meaningful supply and demand.
- Allow prices to evolve naturally.

---

## Design Rules

- NPCs never dominate the market.
- Most items should be created by players.
- The marketplace is primarily player-to-player.
- Players decide what to produce.
- Production should always create economic opportunities.

---

## Why

A player-driven economy creates endless gameplay without requiring endless content.

Instead of developers generating progression, players generate progression for one another.

---

# Pillar 02 â€” Freedom of Choice

## Statement

Players decide how they want to play.

The game never forces a specific profession or playstyle.

---

## Design Goals

Players should be free to become:

- Farmers
- Hunters
- Blacksmiths
- Builders
- Merchants
- Explorers
- Guild members
- Land owners

or any combination they choose.

---

## Design Rules

- No mandatory professions.
- No fixed class system.
- No permanent profession selection.
- Players may change priorities at any time.
- Every profession should remain viable.

---

## Why

Freedom increases replayability.

Different players naturally create different economic ecosystems.

---

# Pillar 03 â€” Meaningful Specialization

## Statement

A player can do many things.

A specialist should always perform a specific activity more efficiently.

---

## Design Goals

Reward dedication without restricting freedom.

Players should naturally develop economic identities.

---

## Design Rules

- Profession experience increases efficiency.
- Better tools increase productivity.
- Knowledge becomes valuable.
- Specialists create market demand.

---

## Why

If every player can efficiently produce everything, trading becomes unnecessary.

Specialization creates dependency.

Dependency creates economy.

---

# Pillar 04 â€” Lands Create Opportunities

## Statement

Land ownership increases production opportunities.

It never creates direct combat superiority.

---

## Design Goals

NFT lands should improve productivity.

Not player power.

---

## Design Rules

Every player receives:

- One free Starter Land.

Starter Lands:

- Cannot receive production bonuses.
- Cannot be traded.
- Guarantee access to all game systems.

NFT Lands:

- Have different sizes.
- Have unique production bonuses.
- Can be traded.
- Increase efficiency through specialization.

---

## Why

Ownership should reward investment without creating Pay-to-Win mechanics.

---

# Pillar 05 â€” Every Session Creates Progress

## Statement

The player should never feel that time was wasted.

Every gameplay session must generate meaningful progress.

---

## Examples

Progress may include:

- Character XP
- Profession XP
- Resources
- Currency
- Crafted Items
- Better Equipment
- Land Development
- Economic Growth

---

## Design Rules

No activity should exist solely to waste time.

Waiting should support gameplay.

Not replace gameplay.

---

## Why

Players should finish every session feeling productive.

---

# Pillar 06 â€” Cooperation Through Incentives

## Statement

Players cooperate because cooperation is profitable.

Not because the game forces them.

---

## Examples

Players visit other lands because:

- Better bonuses.
- Better production.
- Better opportunities.

Players trade because:

- Specialization exists.
- Resources have value.

Players form guilds because:

- Larger goals become achievable.

---

## Design Rules

Avoid forced cooperation.

Create systems where cooperation is naturally rewarding.

---

## Why

Voluntary cooperation creates healthier communities than mandatory cooperation.

---

# Pillar 07 â€” Production Before Consumption

## Statement

Everything valuable should originate from gameplay.

Resources should enter the economy because players actively produce them.

---

## Examples

Trees are planted.

Animals are raised.

Ore is mined.

Furniture is crafted.

Food is cooked.

Equipment is forged.

---

## Design Goals

Every production chain should involve meaningful player participation.

---

## Why

Production creates professions.

Professions create specialization.

Specialization creates trade.

Trade creates economy.

---

# Pillar 08 â€” Risk Should Create Excitement, Not Frustration

## Statement

The game should reward taking risks without excessively punishing failure.

---

## Examples

Exploration.

Guild warfare.

Monster hunting.

Dangerous regions.

Rare resources.

---

## Design Rules

Players should never permanently lose their lands.

Core progression should remain protected.

Risk should create opportunities.

Not irreversible punishment.

---

## Why

Players are more willing to participate in dangerous content when failure does not destroy months of progress.

---

# Pillar 09 â€” Blockchain Supports the Game

## Statement

Gameplay always comes first.

Blockchain exists to support ownership.

Not to define gameplay.

---

## Design Rules

Blockchain should remain almost invisible during normal gameplay.

NFTs represent ownership.

Not combat strength.

Transactions should be simple.

Gameplay should never require technical blockchain knowledge.

---

## Why

Players join because the game is fun.

Blockchain simply enables trustless ownership.

---

# Pillar 10 â€” Long-Term World

## Statement

The world should continue evolving for years without invalidating previous progression.

---

## Design Goals

Every update should expand the world.

Not replace it.

---

## Design Rules

New professions should integrate into existing production chains.

New regions should create economic opportunities.

New resources should strengthen the economy.

Old content should remain relevant.

---

## Why

Persistent worlds become stronger when previous player effort remains valuable.

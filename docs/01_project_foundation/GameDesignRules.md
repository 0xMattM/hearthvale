# Purpose

This document defines the mandatory design rules that every gameplay system must follow.

Unlike the Core Pillars, which describe the philosophy of the game, these rules describe concrete constraints that ensure consistency across all systems.

Every new feature must comply with these rules before implementation.

---

# Rule Categories

The rules are divided into the following categories:

- Economy
- Player Progression
- Lands
- Professions
- Resources
- Crafting
- Combat
- Multiplayer
- Blockchain
- User Experience

---

# Economy Rules

## GR-001 â€” Every Valuable Item Must Have a Purpose

Every resource, crafted item, tool, material, or consumable introduced into the game must serve at least one meaningful gameplay purpose.

Items must never exist simply to increase content quantity.

---

## GR-002 â€” Every Resource Must Be Consumable

Resources should continuously leave the economy.

Examples include:

- Crafting
- Building
- Cooking
- Repairs
- Fuel
- Animal Feeding
- Future content

Permanent accumulation without consumption creates inflation.

---

## GR-003 â€” Every Resource Must Have Multiple Uses

Resources should participate in several production chains whenever possible.

Iron Ore

- Iron Bars
- Steel Bars
- Building Materials
- Tools
- Weapons
- Machines

---

## GR-004 â€” The Economy Must Never Depend on NPCs

NPCs may provide:

- Starter tools
- Tutorials
- Basic services

NPCs must never become the primary producers or consumers of the economy.

---

## GR-005 â€” Supply and Demand Must Come From Players

The game should influence the economy.

Players should control it.

Developers should avoid artificial price fixing whenever possible.

---

# Progression Rules

## GR-006 â€” Every Session Must Generate Progress

Every gameplay session should reward the player with at least one form of progression.

Examples:

- Character XP
- Profession XP
- Resources
- Currency
- Better Equipment
- Better Production

No session should feel wasted.

---

## GR-007 â€” Progress Should Be Permanent

Temporary setbacks are acceptable.

Permanent loss of long-term progression should be avoided.

Players should never lose:

- Character Levels
- Profession Levels
- Lands

---

## GR-008 â€” Better Players Should Become More Efficient, Not Invincible

Progression increases productivity.

Not absolute power.

Skill and preparation should always matter.

---

# Land Rules

## GR-009 â€” Every Player Owns One Starter Land

Every account permanently receives one Starter Land.

Starter Lands guarantee access to every core gameplay system.

---

## GR-010 â€” Starter Lands Never Become Obsolete

Starter Lands remain useful throughout the lifetime of the game.

Players should never be forced to purchase NFTs.

---

## GR-011 â€” NFT Lands Improve Opportunity

NFT Lands improve:

- Space
- Production Capacity
- Specialization

NFT Lands never grant direct combat advantages.

---

## GR-012 â€” Every Land Has an Identity

Every NFT land should be unique.

Identity may come from:

- Production bonuses
- Size
- Terrain
- Future environmental modifiers

No two lands should feel identical.

---

## GR-013 â€” Every Land Supports Every Profession

All lands can support every profession.

Bonuses improve efficiency.

Bonuses never restrict gameplay.

---

## GR-014 â€” Land Bonuses Should Encourage Movement

Players should naturally visit different lands because they provide different production advantages.

The game should reward exploration of the player economy.

---

# Profession Rules

## GR-015 â€” Every Profession Must Be Viable

No profession should become permanently obsolete.

Every profession should contribute to the economy.

---

## GR-016 â€” Every Profession Should Produce Value

Every profession must create something another player may need.

---

## GR-017 â€” Specialization Must Always Be Rewarded

Players may learn many professions.

Specialists should always outperform generalists in efficiency.

---

## GR-018 â€” Professions Must Interact

No profession should exist in complete isolation.

Production chains should naturally connect professions together.

---

# Resource Rules

## GR-019 â€” Resources Should Be Easy to Understand

Players should immediately recognize:

- What the resource is.
- How it is obtained.
- What it is used for.

---

## GR-020 â€” Resource Generation Should Be Predictable

Players should understand how resources regenerate.

Randomness should add variety.

Not confusion.

---

## GR-021 â€” Resources Should Require Decisions

Producing one resource should often mean not producing another.

Players make strategic decisions with limited land space, energy, and time.

---

# Crafting Rules

## GR-022 â€” Crafting Should Add Value

Crafting should increase the economic value of resources.

Every crafting step should feel meaningful.

Advanced crafting should require:

# Combat Rules

## GR-025 â€” Combat Exists to Support the Economy

Combat provides:

- Resources
- Challenges
- Exploration
- Guild objectives

Combat should not replace economic gameplay.

---

## GR-026 â€” Risk Should Produce Better Rewards

Dangerous activities should provide proportionally greater opportunities.

---

## GR-027 â€” Combat Should Never Destroy Long-Term Progress

Players should never permanently lose:

- Lands
- Profession Progress
- Character Progress

---

# Multiplayer Rules

## GR-028 â€” Every System Should Encourage Interaction

Players should naturally:

- Trade
- Visit lands
- Join guilds
- Share knowledge

The best multiplayer experiences emerge from cooperation.

---

## GR-029 â€” Cooperation Should Never Be Mandatory

Every system should remain playable solo.

Cooperation increases efficiency.

It never blocks progression.

---

## GR-030 â€” Guilds Expand Possibilities

Guilds unlock opportunities.

They should never become mandatory for normal progression.

---

# Blockchain Rules

## GR-031 â€” Blockchain Must Remain Invisible

Players should interact with gameplay.

Not blockchain technology.

Wallet interactions should be minimized.

---

## GR-032 â€” NFTs Represent Ownership

NFTs should represent assets.

Never player power.

---

## GR-033 â€” Web3 Should Improve Trust

Blockchain exists to provide:

- Ownership
- Transparency
- Trading

Gameplay always comes first.

---

# User Experience Rules

## GR-034 â€” Every Action Must Be Understandable

Players should always know:

- What happened.
- Why it happened.
- What happens next.

---

## GR-035 â€” Complexity Should Come From Decisions

Never from interface complexity.

The UI should simplify complex systems.

---

## GR-036 â€” Waiting Should Create Planning

Waiting should encourage players to perform different activities.

Waiting should never become the gameplay itself.

# Final Statement

These rules exist to protect the identity of the game throughout its development.

Every new mechanic should strengthen the existing systems rather than compete with them.

Consistency is one of the project's greatest strengths.

Whenever a design decision is uncertain, these rules take precedence over personal preference.

---

# Purpose

The Early Game Experience defines the first interactions a new player has with the game.

The objective is to introduce the core gameplay loop gradually while allowing players to experience the feeling of economic progression from the beginning.

The first hours should communicate the game's main fantasy:

"Start with almost nothing and build your own economic path."

---

# Early Game Philosophy

## Simple Beginning, Deep Future

The player starts with:

- A small free land.
- Basic tools.
- Limited energy.
- Basic knowledge.

The world begins simple, but every system expands naturally.

---

## Teach Through Action

Players should learn by playing.

Avoid long tutorials explaining every system.

The best tutorial is:

- Planting a crop.
- Gathering a resource.
- Crafting an item.
- Selling something.
- Expanding production.

---

# New Player Starting State

Every new player receives:

## Starting Land

A small free land.

Characteristics:

- Limited space.
- No production bonuses.
- Basic building capacity.

Purpose:

Teach land management.

---

## Starting Tools

Basic tools:

- Wooden Axe.
- Wooden Pickaxe.
- Basic Knife.

Purpose:

Allow first gathering activities.

---

## Starting Resources

Small amount of:

- Seeds.
- Basic materials.
- Food.

Purpose:

Allow the first production cycle.

---

# First 5 Minutes

## Objective

Understand movement and interaction.

The player learns:

- How to move.
- How to interact.
- How to access inventory.
- How to view energy.

---

# First 15 Minutes

## Objective

Complete the first production cycle.

Example:

```
Gather Wood

↓

Craft Basic Tool

↓

Plant Crop

↓

Harvest

↓

Sell or Use
```

The player understands:

Actions create value.

---

# First Hour

The player should experience:

## Land Management

The player learns:

- Place production structures.
- Use limited space.
- Choose what to produce.

---

## First Profession

The player naturally starts developing:

Example:

- Farming.
- Mining.
- Woodcutting.
- Hunting.

The game should not force a profession selection.

---

## First Marketplace Interaction

The player learns:

- Check prices.
- Sell resources.
- Buy missing materials.

The marketplace should feel alive from the beginning.

---

# First Day

The player should have:

- A small production setup.
- A profession preference.
- Basic understanding of the economy.

Possible achievements:

- First crafted item.
- First marketplace sale.
- First building.
- First profession level.

# Early Goals

New players should always have clear objectives.

Examples:

- Build your first workshop.
- Reach your first profession level.
- Sell your first product.
- Upgrade your tools.
- Produce your first crafted item.

---

# First Economic Decisions

The player should quickly face meaningful choices.

Example:

"I have limited energy today. Should I:

- Mine iron?
- Harvest crops?
- Cut wood?
- Hunt animals?"

Every choice should have opportunity cost.

---

# Avoiding Early Frustration

The beginning should avoid:

- Long waiting times.
- Expensive requirements.
- Complex menus.
- Empty marketplaces.
- Lack of direction.

---

# New Player Protection

Early players should have:

- Safe areas.
- Accessible resources.
- Affordable tools.
- Basic economic opportunities.

---

# Early Game Monetization Philosophy

The early game should never feel blocked by ownership.

Players without NFTs should still enjoy:

- Production.
- Trading.
- Progression.
- Exploration.

NFT lands provide additional opportunities, not mandatory access.

---

# Success Metrics

A successful early game means players:

- Understand the economy.
- Complete meaningful actions.
- Make their first profit.
- Choose a personal direction.
- Want to continue improving.

---

# Final Statement

The early game transforms a new player from a visitor into an active participant in the economy.

The first hours should create curiosity and confidence while revealing the depth of the world that exists beyond the starting land.

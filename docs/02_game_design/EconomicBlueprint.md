# Economic Blueprint

**Document Version:** 0.1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Game Design Team
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

---

# Purpose

The Economic Blueprint defines the economic architecture of the game.

Every profession, resource, building, crafting recipe, marketplace transaction, and gameplay loop must follow the principles established in this document.

This document serves as the foundation for every future design decision.

Whenever new content is added, it should strengthen the existing economy rather than create isolated gameplay.

---

# Vision

The economy is the core gameplay.

Combat, farming, crafting, gathering, trading, exploration, and land management all exist to produce, transform, exchange, and consume resources.

Players should never feel forced into a specific profession.

Instead, the economy should naturally guide players toward opportunities created by supply and demand.

---

# Core Principles

## Player-Driven Economy

The economy belongs to the players.

Developers introduce systems.

Players determine value.

Supply and demand should define prices.

Developers should rarely intervene directly in the market.

---

## Production Creates Wealth

Wealth is generated through production.

Players create value by:

- Gathering resources
- Processing materials
- Crafting products
- Trading goods
- Investing in land
- Optimizing production

Currency alone does not create wealth.

Products do.

---

## Infinite Resources, Limited Production

Natural resources regenerate.

Scarcity comes from production capacity rather than resource availability.

Production is limited by:

- Energy
- Time
- Land size
- Land specialization
- Profession levels
- Player decisions

This ensures the economy remains sustainable over time.

---

## Every Resource Has a Purpose

No resource should exist without meaningful demand.

Each resource must answer five questions:

- Who produces it?
- Who consumes it?
- How is it transformed?
- What is it used for?
- How does it leave the economy?

If any answer is missing, the resource should be redesigned.

---

## Every Profession Needs Customers

Every profession should provide value to other players.

A profession that produces items nobody needs has failed.

Professions should depend on one another.

Interdependence creates trade.

Trade creates the economy.

---

## Every Item Should Circulate

Items should continuously move through the economy.

A typical lifecycle is:

Resource

↓

Processing

↓

Crafting

↓

Marketplace

↓

Player

↓

Consumption

↓

New Demand

Items that never leave circulation eventually lose value.

---

## Consumption Is Healthy

Consumption is essential.

Resources should continuously leave the economy through:

- Crafting
- Construction
- Durability
- Food
- Consumables

Consumption generates demand.

Demand generates production.

Production generates gameplay.

---

## Freedom Through Specialization

Players should be free to specialize.

No profession should outperform every other profession.

Profitability should fluctuate naturally according to the market.

This encourages diversity without artificial restrictions.

---

# Economic Loop

The fundamental gameplay loop is:

Explore

↓

Gather Resources

↓

Process Materials

↓

Craft Goods

↓

Trade

↓

Earn Currency

↓

Expand Production

↓

Repeat

Every gameplay activity should support this loop.

---

# Production Chain Philosophy

Every crafted item should originate from multiple professions.

Example:

Tree

↓

Logs

↓

Planks

↓

Workbench

↓

Furniture

↓

Marketplace

↓

Player

This creates economic interaction between professions.

---

# Resource Flow

Resources move through the economy in three stages.

## Generation

Resources enter the economy.

Examples:

- Mining
- Farming
- Hunting
- Forestry

---

## Transformation

Resources become more valuable.

Examples:

Iron Ore

↓

Iron Bars

↓

Sword

---

## Consumption

Resources permanently leave the economy.

Examples:

- Building construction
- Tool durability
- Food consumption
- Crafting recipes

---

# Currency Flow

The economy contains two currencies.

## Soft Currency

Used for everyday gameplay.

Examples:

- Marketplace trading
- NPC purchases
- Construction
- Crafting

Obtained entirely through gameplay.

---

## Blockchain Token

Used for blockchain-related features.

Examples:

- NFT land trading
- Premium services
- Future governance

The token should complement gameplay rather than replace it.

---

# Land Economy

Lands increase production capacity.

Lands do not create resources automatically.

Players must actively use land.

Every land represents an investment opportunity rather than passive income.

---

# Marketplace Philosophy

The Marketplace reflects the economy.

It does not control it.

Prices emerge naturally through player behavior.

Developers should avoid fixed pricing whenever possible.

---

# Economic Balance

Healthy economies maintain balance between:

- Resource generation
- Resource consumption
- Currency generation
- Currency sinks
- Production capacity
- Player demand

Balance should emerge naturally whenever possible.

---

# Design Rules

Every new feature should answer the following questions:

Does it create production?

Does it increase interaction?

Does it strengthen specialization?

Does it generate demand?

Does it remove resources from circulation?

Does it encourage trading?

If the answer is "no" to most questions, the feature should be reconsidered.

---

# Future Expansion

Future systems should reinforce the existing economy.

Examples include:

- Trade contracts
- Regional markets
- Transportation systems
- Guild logistics
- Seasonal production
- Dynamic world events

Expansion should deepen the economy rather than replace it.

---

# Final Statement

The economy is the foundation of the game.

Every mechanic ultimately exists to support production, specialization, trade, and cooperation.

A successful economy is not measured by the amount of currency players own, but by the continuous movement of goods, services, and opportunities between players.

When players depend on one another to succeed, the world becomes alive.

# Purpose

The Gameplay Loop defines how players interact with the game over different time scales.

Rather than focusing on individual mechanics, this document describes the continuous cycle of decision-making, production, progression, and economic interaction that forms the core gameplay experience.

Every system introduced into the game should reinforce one or more of these loops.

---

# Design Philosophy

The game is not about reaching an ending.

It is about continuously improving production, discovering new opportunities, and adapting to an ever-changing player-driven economy.

Players should always have meaningful decisions to make.

The goal is not to complete the game.

The goal is to build something greater than yesterday.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

The fundamental gameplay cycle is:

```
Plan

↓

Travel

↓

Gather Resources

↓

Process Materials

↓

Craft

↓

Trade

↓

Earn Currency

↓

Expand Production

↓

Improve Efficiency

↓

Repeat
```

Every profession follows this structure, although the activities performed within each step differ.

---

# The Decision Loop

The most important gameplay loop happens before the player performs any action.

Every session begins with a question:

**What is currently the most profitable use of my time?**

The answer depends on:

- Market prices
- Personal goals
- Profession levels
- Available energy
- Land specialization
- Current inventory

This constant decision-making keeps gameplay fresh.

---

# Daily Session Loop

A typical gameplay session may look like:

1. Check Marketplace prices.
2. Decide today's objective.
3. Travel to the required locations.
4. Gather missing materials.
5. Process raw resources.
6. Craft products.
7. Sell finished goods.
8. Upgrade land or equipment.
9. Prepare for the next session.

The exact activities vary depending on the player's chosen profession.

---

# Long-Term Progression Loop

Over weeks and months, players continuously improve their ability to produce value.

Typical progression includes:

- Unlocking new professions.
- Increasing profession levels.
- Purchasing larger lands.
- Expanding production.
- Crafting better tools.
- Producing higher-value goods.
- Participating in guild activities.
- Competing for territories.

The game should reward long-term planning rather than repetitive grinding.

---

# Profession Loop

Every profession follows the same high-level cycle.

```
Acquire Inputs

↓

Produce

↓

Process

↓

Sell or Use

↓

Earn Profit

↓

Invest

↓

Increase Production

↓

Repeat
```

---

# Land Development Loop

Player lands continuously evolve.

```
Earn Currency

↓

Purchase New Production Structures

↓

Increase Production Capacity

↓

Generate More Goods

↓

Earn More Currency

↓

Expand Further
```

Lands are long-term investments rather than static assets.

---

# Economic Loop

The economy continuously connects players.

```
One Player Produces

↓

Another Player Crafts

↓

Another Player Consumes

↓

Demand Increases

↓

Production Becomes Profitable

↓

More Players Produce
```

This self-regulating cycle is the heart of the game.

---

# Combat Loop

Combat exists as another production activity.

```
Prepare Equipment

↓

Travel

↓

Hunt

↓

Collect Materials

↓

Sell or Craft

↓

Repair Equipment

↓

Repeat
```

Combat feeds the economy instead of replacing it.

---

# Construction Loop

Construction represents permanent investment.

```
Gather Materials

↓

Craft Components

↓

Build Structures

↓

Increase Production

↓

Generate More Resources

↓

Expand Again
```

Construction permanently removes resources from circulation while increasing production efficiency.

---

# Exploration Loop

Exploration creates opportunity.

```
Explore

↓

Discover Resources

↓

Find Better Opportunities

↓

Gather Valuable Materials

↓

Return to Town

↓

Trade
```

Exploration should always have an economic purpose.

---

# Social Loop

Players naturally interact through the economy.

```
Trade

↓

Meet Players

↓

Join Guild

↓

Coordinate Activities

↓

Increase Efficiency

↓

Expand Influence
```

---

# Session Goals

Every player session should accomplish at least one meaningful objective.

Examples include:

- Crafting a new tool.
- Selling valuable goods.
- Expanding a farm.
- Unlocking a profession level.
- Purchasing a new land.
- Building a production structure.
- Winning a territory battle.
- Completing a major trade.

Players should never feel that a session was wasted.

---

# Player Motivation

The game continuously provides new goals through:

- Better efficiency.
- Higher production.
- Larger lands.
- New crafting recipes.
- Market opportunities.
- Territory competition.
- Guild cooperation.
- Economic specialization.

There should always be another objective on the horizon.

---

# Downtime

Waiting is acceptable only when players have meaningful alternatives.

Examples:

- Crops are growing.
- Smelting is processing.
- Animals are reproducing.

During this time, players may:

- Hunt monsters.
- Gather resources.
- Trade.
- Craft.
- Explore.
- Visit other lands.

The game should encourage activity rather than idle waiting.

---

# Success Metrics

A successful gameplay loop encourages players to:

- Make meaningful decisions.
- Interact with the economy.
- Specialize.
- Cooperate.
- Experiment.
- Adapt to market conditions.

The objective is not maximizing playtime.

The objective is maximizing meaningful engagement.

---

# Final Statement

The Gameplay Loop is the foundation of the player experience.

Every mechanic should reinforce a continuous cycle of planning, production, investment, and interaction.

The game succeeds when players finish each session feeling that they made progress, improved their production, and identified exciting opportunities for their next return.

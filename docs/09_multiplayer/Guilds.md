# Purpose

The Guild System allows players to organize into communities that cooperate economically, socially, and militarily.

Guilds are designed to strengthen player interaction, coordinate large-scale production, and participate in Territory Control.

Guilds should create opportunities for collaboration without making solo play unviable.

---

# Vision

A guild should feel like a company, a kingdom, or a community rather than simply a chat group.

Some guilds may specialize in commerce.

Others may become military powers.

Others may dominate specific industries.

Every guild should develop its own identity.

---

# Design Goals

The Guild System must:

- Encourage cooperation.
- Support large-scale production.
- Enable territory wars.
- Create long-term social goals.
- Strengthen the player-driven economy.
- Never make guild membership mandatory.

---

### GUILD-005

---

### GUILD-006

Guild leadership manages membership and permissions.

---

# Guild Philosophy

Guilds exist to organize players.

They do not replace individual progression.

A successful solo player should remain competitive.

Guilds simply allow larger objectives to be achieved more efficiently.

---

# Guild Creation

Creating a guild requires:

- Guild Name
- Guild Tag
- Guild Founder

Future versions may require a small creation fee to reduce inactive guilds.

---

# Membership

Each player belongs to one guild at a time.

Players are free to leave or join another guild.

Future versions may include a cooldown after leaving a guild to prevent abuse during wars.

---

# Guild Roles

Minimum roles include:

## Leader

Full administrative permissions.

---

## Officer

Invites members.

Manages permissions.

Coordinates activities.

---

## Member

Standard guild participation.

---

## Recruit

Optional probationary role.

Limited permissions.

---

# Guild Progression

Guilds do not gain traditional levels in Version 1.

Their growth comes from:

- Active members.
- Territory control.
- Economic influence.
- Reputation.

Future versions may introduce guild achievements or milestones.

---

# Guild Communication

Guild members have access to:

- Guild Chat.
- Member List.
- Online Status.

Future additions may include:

- Announcements.
- Event Calendar.
- Strategy Board.
- Voice integration.

---

# Guild Economy

Guilds do not own player resources.

Every player maintains ownership of:

- Personal inventory.
- Personal lands.
- Personal currency.

Economic cooperation remains voluntary.

---

# Guild Activities

Examples include:

- Territory Wars.
- Group Hunting.
- Resource Gathering.
- Trading.
- Large Construction Projects.
- Community Events.

Guilds provide organization rather than exclusive gameplay.

---

# Relationship with Lands

Player lands remain privately owned.

Guilds never gain ownership of personal lands.

Players decide whether to allow guild members to visit or produce on their lands according to the land permission system.

---

# Relationship with Economy

Guilds increase economic efficiency through coordination.

Examples:

- Shared production planning.
- Resource exchange.
- Profession specialization.
- Market cooperation.

Guilds should improve logistics without bypassing economic rules.

---

# Relationship with Marketplace

Guild members use the same Marketplace as every other player.

There is no exclusive guild marketplace in Version 1.

The economy remains unified.

---

# Relationship with Territory Control

Guilds are the primary participants in Territory Control.

Territories provide temporary strategic and economic advantages.

Guild ownership never affects personal lands.

Detailed mechanics are defined in TerritoryControl.md.

---

# Relationship with Combat

Guilds coordinate players during:

- Territory Wars.
- Boss Hunts.
- Large Expeditions.

Combat remains optional.

Economic-focused guilds should also be viable.

---

# Anti Pay-to-Win

Guild strength comes from:

- Organization.
- Leadership.
- Activity.
- Coordination.

Not from financial investment.

Owning multiple NFT lands does not increase guild power directly.

---

# Risks

## Risk

Solo players feel excluded.

### Mitigation

Ensure all core gameplay systems remain fully accessible without joining a guild.

---

## Risk

Large guilds dominate permanently.

### Mitigation

Use temporary territory ownership and recurring competitions.

---

## Risk

Guilds become inactive.

### Mitigation

Encourage regular activities through Territory Control and community events.

---

Potential future additions include:

---

# Final Statement

Guilds provide the social foundation of the game.

They enable players to coordinate production, organize large-scale activities, and compete for strategic objectives while respecting the game's player-driven economy and individual ownership.

A successful guild is defined not by its size, but by its ability to cooperate, organize, and adapt.

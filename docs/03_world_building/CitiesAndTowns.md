# Purpose

The City System defines the only shared urban center in the world.

The city is the main meeting point for all players and acts as the central hub of:

- Trading.
- Social interaction.
- Player communication.
- Basic services.
- World information.

---

# Design Philosophy

## One World, One City

The game contains a single main city shared by every player.

The purpose is to create:

- A recognizable social center.
- Constant player activity.
- A strong economic hub.
- A place where the community gathers.

---

# The City Role

The city is not a production center.

Players do not create the main economy through NPCs.

The economy is created by:

- Player lands.
- Player production.
- Player crafting.
- Player trading.

The city only connects these systems.

---

# City Areas

The city contains several functional districts.

---

# Marketplace District

The economic center of the world.

Players use it to:

- Sell resources.
- Buy materials.
- Trade crafted items.
- Search opportunities.

---

## Marketplace Rules

The marketplace is fully player-driven.

Prices are determined by:

- Supply.
- Demand.
- Player activity.

NPCs do not control prices.

---

# NPC District

NPCs provide support functions.

Examples:

## Beginner Merchant

Provides:

- Basic tools.
- Starting materials.

Purpose:

Help new players begin.

---

## Service NPCs

Possible services:

- Repair.
- Information.
- Tutorials.

---

# Crafting District

A social crafting area.

Contains:

- Public workstations.
- Demonstration areas.
- Player interaction spaces.

Advanced production remains on player lands.

---

# Social Areas

Places designed for player interaction.

Examples:

- Meeting square.
- Notice boards.
- Rankings.
- Events.

---

# City Entry Point

The city is the first major destination for new players.

The first gameplay loop:

```
Start On Free Land

↓

Produce First Resources

↓

Visit City

↓

Sell / Buy

↓

Improve Production

↓

Return To Land
```

---

# City And Economy

The city connects all professions.

Example:

```
Farmer

↓

Produces Food

↓

Sells In City

↓

Hunter Buys Food

↓

Hunts Monsters

↓

Sells Materials

↓

Blacksmith Creates Equipment
```

---

# Player Activity

The city should always feel alive.

Possible activities:

- Trading.
- Meeting players.
- Discussing opportunities.
- Showing rare items.
- Recruiting guild members.

---

# Safe Zone

The city is a protected area.

Inside the city:

- No PvP combat.
- No resource gathering.
- No land ownership conflicts.

---

# Future Player Features

Possible additions:

- Player shops.
- Guild headquarters.
- Auction events.
- Public competitions.
- Seasonal decorations.

---

# Technical Considerations

For Three.js:

The city should be designed as a high-density area.

Requirements:

- Optimized player rendering.
- Level of detail systems.
- Efficient networking.
- Modular buildings.

---

# Final Statement

The city is the heart of the world.

By having a single shared city, every player contributes to the same economy and community, creating a stronger social experience and a more connected world.

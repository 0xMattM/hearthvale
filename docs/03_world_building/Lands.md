# Purpose

The Land System is the foundation of the game's economy.

Every player owns at least one land where production takes place.

Lands are not cities.

Lands are production spaces.

Players use them to build their own economic strategy.

Unlike traditional farming games, lands are not isolated experiences.

Players are encouraged to visit many different lands because each one offers unique production advantages.

---

# Vision

Every land should feel unique.

Players should not ask:

> "Which land is bigger?"

Instead they should ask:

> "Which land is better for what I want to produce?"

Land specialization creates movement.

Movement creates interaction.

Interaction creates economy.

---

# Design Goals

The Land System must:

- Create production opportunities.
- Encourage specialization.
- Encourage player interaction.
- Increase long-term progression.
- Give NFTs real utility.
- Never become Pay-to-Win.

# Land Size

The physical size of a land determines how many production structures can be placed.

Example progression

| Type | Size |
|-------|------|
| Starter | Small |
| Common | Medium |
| Rare | Large |
| Epic | Huge |
| Legendary | Massive |

Exact dimensions will be defined during balancing.

The important principle is:

More space equals more production choices.

Not more player power.

---

# Production Areas

Players decide how to allocate their available space.

Possible production areas include:

- Crop Fields
- Animal Pens
- Tree Plantations
- Mines
- Carpenter Stations
- Blacksmith Stations
- Furnaces
- Kitchens
- Textile Stations
- Alchemy Stations
- Storage
- Decoration

Every square used for one activity becomes unavailable for another.

Space is one of the game's primary strategic resources.

---

# Land Identity

Every NFT Land has its own production profile.

Example

Agriculture +18%

Mining -12%

Forestry +6%

Cooking +10%

Animal Farming +3%

Bonuses encourage specialization.

They never prevent production.

---

# Production Bonuses

Bonuses affect efficiency.

Possible examples include:

- Faster production
- Higher yield
- Lower energy cost
- Faster growth
- Better quality
- Improved crafting speed

The exact bonus types will be documented separately.

---

# Resource Regeneration

Natural resources regenerate automatically.

Examples include:

Trees regrow.

Animals recover.

Mines replenish.

Crops can be replanted.

Regeneration creates production planning.

It should never encourage idle gameplay.

---

# Shared Lands

Land owners may allow other players to enter.

Visitors may produce resources if permissions allow.

The owner automatically receives an additional production bonus equal to 1% of everything produced on that land.

Example

Visitor harvests:

100 Wheat

Visitor receives:

100 Wheat

Land Owner receives:

+1 Wheat

The visitor never loses production.

The game creates the owner's reward separately.

This system encourages land sharing without feeling like taxation.

---

# Permissions

Every land owner controls access.

Suggested permission levels:

- Private
- Friends
- Guild
- Public

Future permission options may include:

- Building rights
- Storage access
- Crafting access

---

# Strategic Decisions

Every land represents a set of choices.

Should the player:

Increase farming?

Expand mining?

Raise more animals?

Plant additional forests?

Install more crafting stations?

Every expansion has an opportunity cost.

Limited space creates meaningful decisions.

---

# Balance Philosophy

Large lands are more flexible.

Specialized lands are more efficient.

Starter lands remain useful.

No land becomes mandatory.

Every player should always have a productive path forward.

---

# Anti Pay-to-Win Philosophy

Owning more land increases production capacity.

It does not guarantee economic success.

Players still need:

Knowledge.

Planning.

Market understanding.

Profession progression.

Time.

Poor decisions on a large land should perform worse than smart decisions on a small land.

---

# Risks

## Risk

Everyone produces the same resource.

Solution

Different production bonuses.

Limited land space.

Changing market demand.

---

## Risk

Players never visit other lands.

Solution

Unique land bonuses encourage movement.

---

## Risk

Starter lands become useless.

Solution

All gameplay systems remain available on Starter Lands.

Only efficiency differs.

---

## Risk

Large NFT lands dominate the economy.

Solution

Production is limited by:

- Energy
- Time
- Profession Level
- Player Decisions

Not only by available space.

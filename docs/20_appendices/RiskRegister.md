**Document Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Living Document  
**Owner:** Project Management Team

# Purpose

The Risk Register identifies, evaluates, and tracks potential risks that may affect the project's success.

The objective is to:

- Detect risks early.
- Reduce uncertainty.
- Define mitigation strategies.
- Improve decision making.
- Increase project resilience.

---

# Risk Management Philosophy

Risk cannot be completely eliminated.

Instead, the project continuously:

```
Identify

↓

Evaluate

↓

Mitigate

↓

Monitor

↓

Review
```

# Project Risks

---

## PR-001

### Scope Creep

Description

Continuous addition of new features delays the project.

Probability

High

Impact

Critical

Mitigation

- Strict milestone planning.
- Feature freeze before releases.
- Product owner approval for new scope.
- Prioritize MVP first.

---

## PR-002

### Development Delays

Description

Implementation takes longer than expected.

Probability

High

Impact

High

Mitigation

- Smaller milestones.
- Accurate estimation.
- Regular sprint reviews.
- Buffer time for critical tasks.

---

## PR-003

### Resource Constraints

Description

Insufficient developers, artists, or funding.

Probability

Medium

Impact

High

Mitigation

- Modular development.
- Prioritize essential features.
- Outsource non-critical assets.
- Seek partnerships if necessary.

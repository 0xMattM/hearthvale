# Purpose

Animals are an important part of the game's ecosystem.

They provide resources for multiple professions including:

- Animal Breeding
- Hunting
- Cooking
- Textile Production
- Crafting

Animals create economic connections between production, exploration, and player interaction.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Animals Create Value

Animals should not exist only as decoration.

Every animal should provide meaningful resources or gameplay opportunities.

---

## Two Animal Categories

Animals are divided into:

1. Domestic Animals
2. Wild Animals

---

# Domestic Animals

Domestic animals are owned and managed by players on their lands.

They require investment and management.

Players decide whether animal production is profitable based on market conditions.

---

# Animal Breeding System

Players require:

- Animal Pen
- Food
- Water
- Space
- Energy

Animals consume resources over time and generate products.

# Animal Economy

Animals connect several professions.

Example:

Animal Breeder:

Produces Leather

↓

Blacksmith:

Uses Leather

↓

Creates Equipment

↓

Hunter:

Uses Equipment

↓

Produces More Leather

# Purpose

Monsters provide challenging combat activities and introduce rare materials into the economy.

Unlike normal resources, monster materials are obtained through risk, preparation, and combat ability.

Monster hunting creates economic opportunities for players who prefer combat-oriented gameplay.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Combat Supports The Economy

Combat is not a separate game mode.

Monsters exist to provide resources that are valuable for other professions.

Example:

Monster Hunter

↓

Rare Monster Material

↓

Alchemist

↓

Potion

↓

Marketplace

---

## Risk Creates Value

Monster resources should be valuable because obtaining them requires:

- Equipment
- Energy
- Preparation
- Combat skill
- Time

The value comes from player effort.

---

## Monsters Are Not Infinite Money Sources

Monsters should not directly generate large amounts of currency.

Their main purpose is:

- Providing rare materials.
- Creating crafting opportunities.
- Supporting advanced professions.

# Monster Categories

Monsters are divided by difficulty.

---

# Common Monsters

Designed for new players.

Characteristics:

- Low difficulty.
- Basic materials.
- Accessible rewards.

---

# Elite Monsters

Designed for experienced players.

Characteristics:

- Higher difficulty.
- Better materials.
- Require preparation.

---

# Boss Monsters

Large cooperative challenges.

Characteristics:

- Rare appearances.
- Valuable rewards.
- Encourage teamwork.

# Boss System

Bosses are designed as major economic events.

Possible mechanics:

- Large health pools.
- Unique materials.
- Group participation.
- Limited spawn frequency.

---

# Boss Rewards

Rewards should include:

- Rare materials.
- Crafting components.
- Cosmetic rewards.

Avoid:

- Direct currency generation.

---

# Monster Difficulty Scaling

Difficulty can increase through:

- Health
- Damage
- Special abilities
- Required preparation

Rewards should scale with effort.

---

# Monster Respawn

Monsters regenerate naturally.

The objective is not scarcity of monsters.

The objective is limited player capacity through:

- Energy.
- Time.
- Equipment durability.

---

# PvE and Economy Balance

Monster hunting should compete economically with other professions.

Example:

A player should be able to choose:

- Farm for predictable income.
- Mine for resources.
- Hunt monsters for higher risk/reward.

---

# Future Monster Ideas

Possible additions:

- Dragons
- Giants
- Sea Creatures
- Ancient Beasts
- World Bosses
- Regional Bosses

---

# Balance Rules

Every monster should:

- Have a reason to exist.
- Provide useful materials.
- Require preparation.
- Connect to crafting.
- Avoid breaking the economy.

---

# Final Statement

Monsters add risk, adventure, and rare opportunities to the economic world.

The goal is not to create a separate combat game, but to create another profession where players transform effort and risk into valuable resources for the entire economy.

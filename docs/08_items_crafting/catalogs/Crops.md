# Purpose

Crops define the agricultural production system.

Farming is one of the main economic activities available to players and provides essential resources for multiple professions.

Crops create continuous demand through food production, animal care, textile production, and alchemy.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Farming Is A Strategic Choice

Players decide how to use their limited land space.

A player may focus on:

- Food production.
- Textile materials.
- Alchemy ingredients.
- Animal feed.

Different crops create different economic opportunities.

# Live in game (2026-09-01)

Plantable on crop plots (buy seeds at City / Land / Explore vendors; starter bag includes wheat + a little corn):

| Crop | Grow | Harvest sell (Land) | Sinks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wheat | 3 min | 2c | Mill flour → bread / stew |
| Corn | 2 min | 1c | Mill cornmeal → cornbread; harvest stew |
| Potato | 4 min | 3c | Roast potato; harvest stew |
| Cotton | 5 min | 3c | Weave cloth at the loom |
| Herb | 6 min | 5c | Brew herbal tonic |

Explore mines (and one City copper rock) also chip **copper** (60s) and **gold** (120s) besides iron. Forge smelts bars; copper wraps a hoe or nails a crate; gold gilds a fine hoe.

# Final Statement

Agriculture is one of the foundations of the player-driven economy.

Crops create essential resources that connect farmers with cooks, breeders, alchemists, and other professions.

A strong farming system gives players a reliable economic path while continuously generating demand throughout the world.

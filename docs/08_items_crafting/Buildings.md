**Document Version:** 0.1.0

---

# Purpose

The Building System defines every placeable structure that players can construct on their lands.

Buildings are not primarily decorative.

They exist to enable production, processing, storage, and land specialization.

Every building should have a clear economic purpose.

---

# Vision

A player's land should tell the story of their economic strategy.

One land may be filled with crop fields and irrigation.

Another may contain furnaces, forges, and storage.

Another may focus entirely on livestock.

Buildings should represent investment decisions rather than cosmetic progression.

---

# Design Goals

The Building System must:

- Expand production possibilities.
- Consume meaningful resources.
- Create long-term progression.
- Encourage specialization.
- Reward planning.
- Preserve player freedom.

---

# Functional Requirements

### BUILD-001

Buildings may only be placed on player-owned lands.

---

### BUILD-002

Every building occupies land space.

---

### BUILD-003

Buildings require construction materials.

---

### BUILD-004

Construction consumes energy.

---

### BUILD-005

Buildings can be removed by the owner.

---

### BUILD-006

Removing a building should not fully refund construction materials.

---

### BUILD-007

Buildings never become NFTs.

Only lands are NFTs.

---

# Building Philosophy

Buildings unlock production.

They do not produce value by themselves.

Value comes from players using them.

A forge without a blacksmith produces nothing.

A field without crops produces nothing.

A pasture without animals produces nothing.

# Land Space

Buildings consume valuable land space.

Every placement represents an opportunity cost.

Building one additional forge means less space for crops, trees, or animals.

Players should constantly evaluate how to optimize their land.

---

# Construction Progression

As players progress they unlock:

- More advanced stations.
- Greater efficiency.
- Larger storage.
- Specialized infrastructure.

Progression should expand possibilities rather than replace previous structures.

# Relationship with Lands

Buildings define how land is used.

Two players owning identical lands may create completely different production layouts.

This reinforces specialization.

---

# Relationship with Energy

Construction consumes energy.

Large projects require greater investment.

Energy prevents unlimited expansion.

---

# Durability

Version 1 does not include building durability.

Buildings remain permanently functional after construction.

Future maintenance systems may be introduced if they improve the economy without creating unnecessary frustration.

---

# Upgrades

Version 1 does not include direct building upgrades.

Instead, players replace older structures with improved versions.

This keeps the system simple.

Future versions may support upgrades if they provide meaningful gameplay.

---

# Anti Pay-to-Win

Buildings require:

- Resources
- Energy
- Professions
- Time

Owning larger lands provides more space but does not remove these requirements.

Production still depends on player decisions.

---

# Risks

## Risk

Players build everything.

### Mitigation

Limited land space.

Construction costs.

Energy requirements.

---

## Risk

Some buildings become mandatory.

### Mitigation

Provide multiple viable production paths.

Avoid single points of progression.

---

## Risk

Decorations become useless.

### Mitigation

Keep decorations purely cosmetic.

Never require them for progression.

---

## Risk

Buildings feel permanent and discourage experimentation.

### Mitigation

Allow removal at any time.

Only partially refund materials.

# Open Questions

Should roads improve movement speed?

Should irrigation affect crop growth?

Should warehouses increase local storage only?

Should production stations process multiple recipes simultaneously?

Should some advanced structures require rare materials?

---

# Final Statement

Buildings are the physical expression of a player's economic strategy.

Every structure represents an investment of resources, space, and planning.

Rather than providing automatic value, buildings enable players to transform effort into production.

The Building System exists to convert land into opportunity while preserving the freedom to specialize, experiment, and adapt to the evolving player-driven economy.
